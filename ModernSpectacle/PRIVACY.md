# Modern Spectacle Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Modern Spectacle does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data.

- **Window information stays on your Mac.** To arrange windows, the app reads the names of open apps and the titles and positions of their windows through the macOS Accessibility API. This information is used only on your Mac. When Apple Intelligence is available, it is passed to Apple's on-device language model, which runs locally; it is not sent over the network.
- **Local files.** Your saved layouts, layout preferences and a short activity log are kept in the app's own folder on your Mac. Deleting the app deletes them.
- **One network request.** Once a day the app asks the developer's store whether a newer version exists; that request sends only the app's version number, and it can be turned off in Settings.
- **No analytics, advertising or tracking.**

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/modenl/spectacle/issues.

# Modern Spectacle 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 22 日

Modern Spectacle 不收集、不上传、不出售、不分享任何个人数据。

- **窗口信息只留在你的 Mac 上。** 为了排列窗口，应用通过 macOS 辅助功能接口读取已打开应用的名称，以及窗口的标题和位置。这些信息只在本机使用；Apple Intelligence 可用时，会交给在本机运行的 Apple 模型判断，不经过网络。
- **本地文件。** 保存的布局、布局偏好和简短的运行日志保存在应用自己的文件夹里，删除应用即一并删除。
- **只有一个网络请求。** 应用每天向开发者的商店检查一次是否有新版本，请求里只有版本号，可以在设置里关闭。
- **没有统计、广告或追踪。**

问题请在 https://github.com/modenl/spectacle/issues 提出。
