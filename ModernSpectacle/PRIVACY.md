# Modern Spectacle Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Modern Spectacle does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data.

- **Window information.** To arrange windows, the app reads the names of open apps and the titles and positions of their windows through the macOS Accessibility API. If you enter a TypeSafe (Jev) key in Settings, each Smart Arrange sends the app names, window titles and recent window switches to TypeSafe's API (api.typesafe.ai) to decide which windows matter; window positions are not sent. Without a key, nothing leaves your Mac. The key is kept in your macOS Keychain.
- **Local files.** Your saved layouts, layout preferences and a short activity log are kept in the app's own folder on your Mac. Deleting the app deletes them.
- **Update check.** Once a day the app asks the developer's store whether a newer version exists; that request sends only the app's version number, and it can be turned off in Settings.
- **No analytics, advertising or tracking.**

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/modenl/spectacle/issues.

# Modern Spectacle 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 23 日

Modern Spectacle 不收集、不上传、不出售、不分享任何个人数据。

- **窗口信息。** 为了排列窗口，应用通过 macOS 辅助功能接口读取已打开应用的名称，以及窗口的标题和位置。如果你在设置里填了 TypeSafe（Jev）Key，每次智能排列会把应用名、窗口标题和最近的窗口切换发给 TypeSafe 的接口（api.typesafe.ai），由它判断哪些窗口重要；窗口位置不会发送。没有 Key 时，任何内容都不离开你的 Mac。Key 保存在 macOS 钥匙串里。
- **本地文件。** 保存的布局、布局偏好和简短的运行日志保存在应用自己的文件夹里，删除应用即一并删除。
- **更新检查。** 应用每天向开发者的商店检查一次是否有新版本，请求里只有版本号，可以在设置里关闭。
- **没有统计、广告或追踪。**

问题请在 https://github.com/modenl/spectacle/issues 提出。
